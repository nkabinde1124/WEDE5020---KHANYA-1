our story

From a garage in Richard's Bay to a tailoring house trusted across Richard's Bay

How it started

Stitch and Seam began in 2014 when founder Thandiwe Mokoena started altering school uniforms out her homegarage. Word spread quickly, and soon clients were comming for more than hems and buttons they wanted full custom suits, traditional wear, and wedding attire

How it grew

By 2018, the business had outgrown the garage completly and moved into a proper studio space. What started as one woman operation is now a team of five skilled tailors,all carrying the same care into every stitch

Our mission

To provide expertly tailored clothing that fits every body perfectly, blending traditional craftsmanship with modern style-one stitch at a time
