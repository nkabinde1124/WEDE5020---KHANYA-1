Website goals and objectives:

website goals-provide customers with clear information about stitch and seam tailoring , Show the customers the offered tailoring services and their prices , Facilitate the making of booking enquiries on the website , provide the customers with the opportunity to get ACCESS to the contact information of the business at all times , attract more customers , Provide a website that is easy to navigate and is user friendly.

Website objectives-Increase awareness for stitch and seam tailoring , Help customers in finding the required information about the services , Help customers in booking appointments or tailoring services , minimize time needed for getting in touch with the business for basic information as it will be available on the website ,Provided an easy navigation throughout the website pages.

Key features and functionality- The website will have five main pages home page, about page, services page, booking page, contact page. The home page will give a brief overview of the business, about page will go into detail on everything about the business such as when it started, services page will detail what is offered by the business , booking page will allow customers to book appointments and other things, contact page will list all the ways customers can get in touch with the business ways like social media platform, email address and phone numbers.

timeline and milestone: The development process will be divided into different stages week 1-all research will be conducted on the target audience, goals will be defined week 2-structure and wireframes for the website will be created, the about page and home page will be created week 3-booking page and services page will be completed, contact page will be completed week 4-visual aspects of the website like images, styling and branding will be completed, website will be tested by this point week 5-final reviews will be done, final troubleshooting will be done, documentation will be done.
